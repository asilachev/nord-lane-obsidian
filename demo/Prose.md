# A longer read

With `cssclasses: [prose]`, paragraphs stop at a comfortable measure, about ninety-five characters, while the table below keeps the full column. Long lines tire the eye: it has to travel far to the right and then find the start of the next line again, and on a wide pane one paragraph can turn into two or three very long lines.

- Lists follow the same measure.
- So do headings and quotes.

| Section | Words | Minutes | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Opening | 420 | 2 | sets the question |
| Middle | 1,380 | 6 | three examples, one counterexample |
| Close | 310 | 1 | what to try next |

> A quote stays at the reading measure too, so the eye doesn't have to jump.

Everything shares the column's left edge, so the page still reads as one column.
